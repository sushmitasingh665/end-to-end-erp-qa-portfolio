# Consignment Orders

Consignment represents inventory movement from a source location to a destination location.

## Validation points
- Verify source and destination locations.
- Verify the intended product and quantity.
- Validate shipment processing.
- Validate the posted transfer receipt at the destination.
- Verify inventory movement between locations.

**Expected business distinction:** consignment completion is a transfer/delivery process, not a normal sales invoice process. Do not expect a sales invoice PDF for this flow.
