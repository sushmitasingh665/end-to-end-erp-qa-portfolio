# Requisitions and Approvals

## Known scope
- Create requisition orders in Navision.
- Validate procurement setup and configured approver stages.
- Follow multi-level approval as configured.
- Verify status changes and the correct approver at each stage.

## QA coverage
- Valid requisition submission.
- Missing mandatory data.
- Approval and rejection.
- Multi-level routing.
- Unauthorized approval attempt.
- Re-submission after correction, where supported.

Do not assume a requisition-to-purchase-order transition until the exact business rule and configuration are confirmed.
