# Purchase Invoices and Payment Advice

## Known scope
- Purchase invoice may be posted automatically or manually, depending on configuration.
- Payment advice is created and submitted for approval.
- After approval, a payment advice document is generated and emailed.

## QA coverage
- Correct invoice reference and amount.
- Automatic/manual posting path.
- Payment advice creation.
- Approval routing and rejection.
- Generated document content and recipient validation.
- Email notification delivery, using a controlled test mailbox.

Never include real banking details, vendor identifiers, email addresses or payment documents in this public portfolio.
